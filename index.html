Learn statistics and R by poking at shapes.

Short visual puzzles that start much earlier than other tools do. Drag, tap and guess, then see what the maths says. No account, no typing, no scrolling on a phone.

The games

Bag of Shapes · Bayesian statistics

A hidden bag of circles and squares. Draw, count, bet your belief, and watch it update. Starts at "what is a share?" and builds up to priors, posteriors and hierarchical models.

Play →

R from zero · Base R, then tidy R

Build and read code by dragging tiles and following tables as they change. Part 1 is base R. Part 2 is the tidyverse, with the pipe as "and then".

Play →

Models and scales · Regression and mixed models

Learn to read a model table: the scale you are on, what the intercept means, and what random effects do. R is only the tool, you do not type it.

Play →

What is different

  • Starts lower.Each game begins before the textbook does, and a short placement test lets you skip what you know.
  • Visual, not quizzes.You move things to answer. Words for ideas arrive after you have met the idea.
  • Small steps.Five right in a row to finish a level. Two hearts. Nothing to calculate.
  • Yours alone.Progress lives in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
