Well Rounded LearningGames

About

Small browser games for learning statistics, R, syntax and research methods. They build on Shapes and Language and Trial and Error: try things, see what happens.

How they teach

  • Idea first, name second. You meet a concept before you are given its word.
  • One possible move on the first level of anything new.
  • Drag and tap instead of reading and choosing.
  • Checked against real R. Questions are generated so lazy strategies fail.
  • Typical mistakes are the lessons.

Accessibility

Large text, 44px buttons, WCAG contrast, keyboard support, and an automated audit on three phone sizes. If something is hard to use, please say so.

Sources

The Bayesian game follows Nicenboim, Schad and Vasishth, An Introduction to Bayesian Data Analysis for Cognitive Science. The examples and wording are original.

Privacy and licence

No accounts, tracking or server. Progress stays in your browser. MIT licence, source on GitHub.
